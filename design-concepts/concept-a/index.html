DESIGN CONCEPT · 01 / OPS CONSOLE

Structured recon.
AI triage.
One console.

PenTron runs your recon toolkit against an authorized target, hands the output to an LLM, and comes back with a scored, structured report — vulnerabilities, exploit paths and a risk level, next to the raw logs that produced them. This page is a visual exploration of one possible direction for that interface: dense, technical, built for reading logs at 2am.

What PenTron does

One pipeline, from raw tool output to a readable verdict.

tool_registry

nmap, whois, curl, dig, nikto, subfinder and more — every recon module is registered once and picked up everywhere it's needed.

llm_triage

Raw output is parsed into VULN / EXPLOIT / RISK_LEVEL blocks and scored — CRITICAL down to LOW — without manual read-through.

providers

Local Ollama model, or OpenAI / Anthropic / Google — swap the backing provider without changing how a scan is run.

session_report

Target, tools, findings, exploit paths and raw logs stay attached to the session — nothing is summarized away.
